# Business Intelligence Foundations (DVA402)

Core methods for reporting, dashboards, and decision support.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Business intelligence lifecycle**
   Connect source systems, data preparation, analysis, and decision-making.

2. **Metrics and KPI definitions**
   Define measurable indicators with clear owners, formulas, and reporting periods.

3. **Dimensional reporting models**
   Organize measures and dimensions for consistent business analysis.

4. **Dashboard design and governance**
   Build a governed report that supports repeatable operational decisions.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
