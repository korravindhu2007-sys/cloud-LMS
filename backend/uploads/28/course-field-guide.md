# Applied Computing Studio 2 (FAC09982)

A permanent applied computing course for Asha Verma.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Applied systems integration studio**
   Plan the integration of services, data, and operational constraints.

2. **Reliability and validation**
   Test integrated behavior under expected and failure conditions.

3. **Project handoff and technical reflection**
   Prepare a maintainable handoff and reflect on trade-offs.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
