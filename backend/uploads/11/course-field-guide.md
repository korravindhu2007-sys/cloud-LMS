# Human Centered Design (UXD401)

Research-led methods for useful digital products.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Human-centered design process**
   Frame design around user needs, context, and iterative evaluation.

2. **User research methods**
   Select interviews, observation, and surveys to answer a research question.

3. **Synthesis and problem framing**
   Turn research evidence into useful themes, needs, and design opportunities.

4. **Prototyping and accessibility**
   Prototype a solution and check it against inclusive design needs.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
