# Interaction Design Studio (UXD402)

Designing accessible and effective user interactions.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Interaction patterns and feedback**
   Design control behavior and feedback that make system state understandable.

2. **Interaction states and flows**
   Map states, transitions, and error paths in a user journey.

3. **Prototyping interactions**
   Use a prototype to test timing, navigation, and interaction assumptions.

4. **Accessible interaction design**
   Support keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion interaction.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
