# Software Engineering Essentials (SE301)

Modern software delivery practices for collaborative teams.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Requirement gathering**
   Capture stakeholder needs as testable functional and quality requirements.

2. **Design thinking**
   Move from a defined problem through ideas and prototypes to feedback.

3. **Testing strategies**
   Choose unit, integration, and acceptance checks that match the risks of a release.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
