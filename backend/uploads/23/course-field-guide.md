# Quality Assurance Methods (QAE401)

Systematic approaches to software quality.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Quality engineering lifecycle**
   Relate prevention, detection, and feedback to the delivery lifecycle.

2. **Test design and coverage**
   Choose test cases based on risk, boundaries, and expected behavior.

3. **Reviews and defect analysis**
   Use review evidence and defect patterns to improve product quality.

4. **Continuous quality practices**
   Connect automated checks to rapid, reliable delivery feedback.

## Practice
