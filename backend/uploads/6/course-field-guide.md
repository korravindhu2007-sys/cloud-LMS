# Kubernetes Operations (CNE402)

Practical container orchestration and platform operations.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Kubernetes architecture**
   Describe the control plane, worker nodes, and reconciliation loop.

2. **Pods, deployments, and services**
   Deploy replicated workloads and expose stable network endpoints.

3. **Configuration and secrets**
   Separate application configuration from images and protect sensitive values.

4. **Scaling and rollout operations**
   Perform safe rolling updates, rollback, and workload scaling.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
