# Data Engineering Pipelines (DBE402)

Reliable data ingestion, transformation, and delivery.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Data pipeline architecture**
   Separate ingestion, transformation, storage, and delivery responsibilities.

2. **Batch and streaming ingestion**
   Choose an ingestion approach based on latency and throughput needs.

3. **Transformation and orchestration**
   Make pipeline transformations repeatable and schedule dependencies.

4. **Data quality and lineage**
   Validate records and trace data from source through downstream use.
