# Cybersecurity Fundamentals (CYS401)

Security principles, threats, and practical defenses.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Security principles and risk**
   Apply confidentiality, integrity, and availability to practical risk scenarios.

2. **Threat modeling**
   Identify assets, trust boundaries, threats, and mitigations before implementation.

3. **Network defense fundamentals**
   Use segmentation, filtering, and monitoring to reduce network exposure.

4. **Incident response lifecycle**
   Prepare, detect, contain, recover, and learn from a security incident.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
