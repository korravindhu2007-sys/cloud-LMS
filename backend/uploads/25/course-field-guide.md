# Applied Computing Studio 1 (FAC12491)

A permanent applied computing course for CORS Faculty.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Applied systems problem framing**
   Translate a computing scenario into constraints, stakeholders, and measurable goals.

2. **Prototype and evaluate a solution**
   Build a small prototype and evaluate it against explicit requirements.

3. **Technical communication and review**
   Document decisions and use peer review to improve an applied solution.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
