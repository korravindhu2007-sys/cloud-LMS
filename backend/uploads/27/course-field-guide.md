# Applied Computing Studio 1 (FAC09981)

A permanent applied computing course for Asha Verma.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Applied software design studio**
   Explore a real-world software need and define a focused solution scope.

2. **Build a testable prototype**
   Implement a prototype with observable behavior and a small test plan.

3. **Review, refine, and demonstrate**
   Use feedback to refine a solution and communicate its limitations.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
