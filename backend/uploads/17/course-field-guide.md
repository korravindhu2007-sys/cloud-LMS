# Network Architecture (NET401)

Planning reliable network systems and services.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Network models and protocols**
   Relate protocol layers to addressing, transport, and application behavior.

2. **IP addressing and subnetting**
   Plan address ranges and subnet boundaries for a network.

3. **Routing and switching**
   Explain how forwarding decisions move traffic across connected networks.

4. **Resilient network architecture**
   Design redundant paths and capacity for reliable network services.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
