# Data Visualization and Storytelling (DVA401)

Communicating analytical findings through visual narratives.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Choosing visual encodings**
   Match data types and analytical questions to effective visual encodings.

2. **Chart selection and comparison**
   Choose charts that reveal comparisons, distributions, and relationships.

3. **Building a data narrative**
   Arrange evidence and annotations into a clear analytical story.

4. **Dashboard usability**
   Design dashboard hierarchy, filters, and labels for the intended audience.

## Practice

Use the course practice assignment to explain the key ideas in your own words and support your answer with an example.
